Resistance Calculator for running LEDs

A simple tool to calculate the resistor required to control voltage and current for LEDs.

Inputs

Power source  Vsource
LED power  PLED
LED forward voltage  VLED

Results

LED current ILED = PLED / VLED
Voltage to drop ΔV = Vsource − VLED
Required resistance R = ΔV / ILED
Resistor power PR = ΔV × ILED
