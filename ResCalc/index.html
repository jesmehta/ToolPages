Resistance Calculator for running LEDs

A handy tool to calculate the resistor required to control voltage and current for LEDs.

Inputs

Power source  Vsource
LED power  P
LED forward voltage  VLED

Results

LED current ILED = P / VLED
Voltage to drop ΔV = Vsource − VLED
Required resistance R = ΔV / ILED
Resistor power PR = ΔV × ILED

Closest standard resistor (E24, ±5%)

Notes: a resistor is a simple way to limit LED current, but it wastes ΔV × I as heat. For high-power LEDs (1 W and up) a constant-current driver is usually more efficient. The LED forward voltage varies between parts — check your datasheet.
